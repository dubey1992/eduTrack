The everyday challenge

Running a school shouldn't mean juggling five different tools.

When the register, the fee book and the bus list live in separate places, even simple questions take a phone call to answer.

  • 01 / Disconnected records

    Student information, scattered

    Staff spend valuable time hunting for attendance, class details and family contacts.

  • 02 / Manual admin work

    Too much repetitive paperwork

    Fee follow-ups, staff registers, reports and timetables mean the same work done twice.

  • 03 / Missed updates

    Everyone asks for the latest

    Leaders, staff and families need to know what is happening today, without a round of calls.

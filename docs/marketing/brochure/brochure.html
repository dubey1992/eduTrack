The school day

Registers, timetables and teaching, done once and done right.

Student attendance

  • One register per class per day, marked Present, Absent or On leave.
  • Works without signal in the staff app, and sends itself once the phone is online.
  • Parents of absent students told by SMS, WhatsApp or email.
  • Holidays and weekends closed, so nobody marks the wrong day.

Staff attendance and check-in

  • Check in from the phone, only at school - within the school's radius, 100 metres by default.
  • Location is read only at the tap; nothing follows anybody around.
  • Late arrivals worked out; approved leave written onto the register.
  • A forgotten check-out is filled in and flagged for the office.

Timetable, teaching reports, syllabus

  • No double-booking: a teacher can't be in two classes in one period.
  • Several breaks a day, with ready-made presets.
  • One teaching report per period, and the syllabus ticks itself from it.
  • Heads of department review the reports of their own department.

Class tests and progress reports

  • Tests, assignments and quizzes, with marks on the web or the phone.
  • The school's own grade scales and terms.
  • Published results sent to guardians, and polished PDF progress reports.
  • Class promotion into the next year in one pass.
Early Warning

Early warning, before it is too late

Students whose attendance or test results are low or falling are listed with the reason - "attendance 56% over the last 30 school days" - so a class teacher can act this week, not at the end of term.

Fees and payroll

Every rupee in and out, with a receipt for each one.

Student Fees

Fee management

  • Fee plans per class and year - monthly, quarterly, termly, yearly or one-time.
  • Bus fees follow the route a student rides.
  • Cash, bank transfer, UPI, cheque or card, with part payments paying the oldest dues first.
  • Numbered PDF receipts with no gaps, printed or emailed.
  • Fee reminders to parents, and late fines if the school uses them.
  • Discounts with a reason, and a credit balance for money paid in advance.

Payroll and leave

  • A monthly run from draft to finalised to paid.
  • Worked out from attendance: half days, unpaid absence and a mid-month start.
  • PDF payslips, emailed and readable in the staff app.
  • Staff apply for leave from the phone; an admin or their head of department approves it.
  • An Accountant role, and bank details kept encrypted.

In your own currency. Each school works in its own currency - rupees, dollars, naira and more - and every amount, receipt and payslip carries it. Reports never blend currencies together.

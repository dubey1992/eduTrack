The school day

Registers, timetables and teaching, done once and done right.

Student attendance

  • One register per class per day, marked Present, Absent or On leave.
  • The register opens with everyone present: mark the absent ones with a swipe, or find a child by name or roll number.
  • Works without signal in the staff app, and sends itself once the phone is online.
  • Parents of absent students told by SMS, WhatsApp or email; holidays and weekends closed.

Staff attendance and check-in

  • Check in from the phone, only at school - within the school's radius, 100 metres by default.
  • Location is read only at the tap; nothing follows anybody around.
  • Late arrivals worked out; approved leave written onto the register.
  • A forgotten check-out is filled in and flagged for the office.

Timetable, teaching reports, syllabus

  • No double-booking: a teacher can't be in two classes in one period.
  • Several breaks a day, with ready-made presets.
  • One teaching report per period - "Same as last time", a template per subject - and the syllabus ticks itself from it.
  • Heads of department review the reports of their own department.

Leave

  • Staff apply for leave from the phone, and can withdraw a request still pending.
  • A School Admin decides, or the head of department for their own department - from the phone too.
  • What each person took this year, at a glance.
Insights · Early Warning

Early warning, before it is too late

Students whose attendance or test results are low or falling are listed with the reason - "attendance 56% over the last 30 school days" - so a class teacher can act this week, not at the end of term.

  • Versioned publishing: change the date sheet and publish version 2 - every card says which version it is.

  • The school's logo and name on every card.

Marks, grades and report cards

  • Class tests, assignments and quizzes, with marks on the web or the phone.
  • Grading by percentage, GPA or grades, with the school's own pass rule and an optional rank.
  • Report cards in a Standard or a Classic printed layout; results sent to guardians.
  • Class promotion into the next year in one pass.

Fees and payroll

Every rupee in and out, with a receipt for each one.

Fee management

  • Fee plans per class and year - monthly, quarterly, termly, yearly or one-time - and bus fees that follow the route.
  • Cash, bank transfer, UPI, cheque or card, with part payments paying the oldest dues first.
  • Numbered PDF receipts with no gaps, printed or emailed.
  • Reminders, late fines, discounts with a reason, and a credit balance for money paid in advance.
  • Collection report by person and by payment mode, and today's takings on the dashboard.
  • A date lock on the fee books: once a period is closed, nothing dated in it can change.

Payroll

  • A monthly run from draft to finalised to paid.
  • Worked out from attendance: half days, unpaid absence and a mid-month start.
  • Staff advances logged once and recovered from each payslip until repaid.
  • PDF payslips, emailed, and readable and shareable in the staff app.
  • An Accountant role, and bank details kept encrypted.

Documents in your school's own style

Every receipt, payslip, report card and admit card carries the school's logo and name. Receipts, report cards and payslips come in a Standard or a Classic printed layout - the school chooses once, in Settings.

Safe, and quick to start

Your data kept safe, and a guided start.

Safe by design

  • Each school's data kept apart. Nobody can see another school's records.
  • The right screens for each role, set by the school - and modules it does not use switched off.
  • Every change recorded in an audit log: who, what and when.
  • Protected sign-in: strong passwords, lockout after repeated wrong tries, sign-out of lost devices.
  • Closed fee books stay closed: the date lock stops changes to a finished period.
  • An app lock on staff phones, with the phone's own fingerprint, face or screen lock.

This article explains how to ask for leave, on the web or in the School365ai staff app, and how the person who decides approves or rejects it.

## Who can do this

Any member of staff with an employment record at the school can apply for leave. A School Admin can approve or reject any request in the school. An HOD can decide requests from the staff in their own department. Nobody can approve their own leave.

## Apply in the staff app

1. Open the app and go to the **Leave** tab.
2. Tap **Apply for leave**.
3. Choose the **Leave type**: Casual Leave, Medical Leave, Earned Leave or Half Day.
4. Pick the dates in **From** and **To**. For a half day, pick the one date.
5. Write a short **Reason**.
6. Tap **Send request**.

Your request shows on the **Leave** tab as Pending until someone decides it.

## Apply on the web

1. Open **Staff Leave** in the sidebar and choose **Apply Leave**.
2. Fill in the **Leave type**, **From**, **To** and **Reason**.
3. Choose **Submit Request**.

## Approve or reject a request

1. Open **Staff Leave** on the web. Pending requests are listed with the person's name, the dates and their reason.
2. Choose **Approve** or **Reject**.

Once a request is approved, those days show as leave on the staff attendance register, so nobody needs to mark them by hand.

## Good to know

- If your school has leave decision alerts switched on, the person who applied is told when their leave is approved or rejected.
- Leave can run across a holiday. Only the working days inside the dates are counted as leave, so the holiday is not used up.
- On the day itself, a teacher's **Home** screen in the staff app shows that they are on leave, and lists any other leave they have that week.

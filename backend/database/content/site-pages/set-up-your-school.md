This article walks you through setting up a new school in School365ai, in an order that saves you going back: academic year, departments, subjects, then classes and sections.

## Who can do this

A School Admin sets up the school. A Group Admin can do the same for each school in their group. You do all of it on the web, from the **Academics** group in the sidebar.

## Before you start

It helps to add your teachers first, because departments, subjects and sections each let you pick a teacher. Go to **Teachers & Staff** and use **Add Employee**. You can also leave those choices empty and fill them in later.

## Steps

1. Open **Academic Years** and choose **Add Academic Year**. Give it a name such as 2026-27, a **Start date** and an **End date**. Tick **Current academic year** if this is the year you are starting in, or use **Set Current** on the list afterwards.
2. While you are there, open **Terms** on the year and use **Add Term** for each term. Class tests and progress reports are grouped by term, so it is worth doing now.
3. Open **Departments** and choose **Add Department**. Enter the **Department name** and, if you know it, pick the **HOD (optional)**.
4. Open **Subjects** and choose **Add Subject**. Enter a **Code (e.g. MAT)** and the **Subject name**, set the **Min class level** and **Max class level** it is taught at, and pick its **Department**. A **Lead teacher (optional)** can be added now or later.
5. Open **Classes & Sections** and choose **Add Class**. Pick the **Academic year**, then enter the class name, for example Grade 8, and its **Level (0-12)**.
6. On each class, choose **Add Section**. Enter the **Section (e.g. A)**, an optional room number, and the **Class teacher (optional)**. Only the class teacher of a section can take its register, so set this before the first school day.

Once these are in place you can add students, build the timetable and start taking attendance. To add many students at once, see [Import students from a spreadsheet](/help/import-students-from-a-spreadsheet).

## Good to know

- Classes belong to an academic year. When the year ends, **Class Promotion** moves a class into the next year, so you do not have to rebuild everything.
- The class level is what links a class to the subjects taught at that level, so use the same numbering for both.
- Add your school holidays under **Holidays**. Attendance, leave and teaching reports all skip those days.
- The [student information system](/student-information-system) page gives an overview of how classes, students and records fit together.

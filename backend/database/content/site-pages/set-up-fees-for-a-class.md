This article shows you how to enter a class's fees for the academic year in School365ai, split them into installments, and publish them so the students in that class are charged.

## Who can do this

A School Admin or an Accountant, on the web, if your school uses fee management. If you do not see **Fees** in the sidebar, ask School365ai to switch it on for your school.

## Steps

1. Open **Fee Setup** in the sidebar and pick the **Academic year** at the top.
2. On the **Fee Types** tab, choose **Add Fee Type** for each kind of fee you charge, such as Tuition or Lab. A Transport fee type is already there for bus fees.
3. Open the **Class Fees** tab. Each class has its own card. On the class you want, choose **Add fee**.
4. Pick the **Fee type** and enter the **Amount for the year**.
5. Choose a **Frequency**: Monthly, Quarterly, Per term, Yearly or One-time. Set the **Due on day** of the month if you want a different day.
6. Use the fill-in button under **Installments** to split the amount for you. Check each installment's name, due date and amount, and change them if you need to. Use **Add installment** to add one by hand.
7. Choose **Save as draft**. A draft charges nobody yet, so you can add the rest of the class's fees first.
8. When the fees look right, choose **Publish** on the fee, or use the button at the top to publish all the year's drafts at once. You are asked to confirm.

When a fee is published, every active student in that class for that year is charged its installments. Students who join the class later are charged automatically when they are added, for the installments due on or after their admission date.

## Bus fees

Fees for a bus route work the same way, on the **Bus Route Fees** tab. Students are charged when they are assigned to that route.

## Good to know

- Amounts are always in your school's own currency.
- After a fee is published, you can still change it with **Edit**. Only installments that are unpaid change. Anything already paid stays as it was.
- To give one student a reduced fee, use **Add Discount** on their statement in **Student Fees** rather than changing the class fee.
- Once fees are charged, see [Record a fee payment and print the receipt](/help/record-a-fee-payment). Read more about [school fee management software](/school-fee-management-software).

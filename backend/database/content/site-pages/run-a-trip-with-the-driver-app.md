This article explains how bus crew run a trip in the School365ai Driver app on an Android phone: signing in, starting the trip, marking each student at each stop, and ending it.

## Who can do this

Drivers and Bus Attendants who are assigned to a route. The school office adds you under **Crew** and gives you a setup code for your phone.

## Set up your phone the first time

1. Open the app and tap **Set up this phone**.
2. Enter your **Mobile** number and the **Setup code** the office gave you.
3. Choose a **New passcode** and type it again in **Confirm passcode**.
4. Tap **Register and sign in**.

After that, you sign in with your mobile number and **Passcode**, then **Sign In**. If you lose your phone or forget your passcode, the school office can give you a new setup code.

## Run the trip

1. On **Home** you see your routes for today. Tap **Start pickup** or **Start drop** on the route you are about to run.
2. When you reach a stop, tap **Reached**.
3. Mark each student at the stop: **Boarded** when they get on, **Dropped** when they get off, or **Absent** if they do not come.
4. If you need to reach a guardian, use **Call parent** beside the student. The app places the call without showing you their number.
5. When the last student is dropped, tap **End trip**.

If you leave the trip screen, tap **Continue pickup** or **Continue drop** on the route to go back to it.

## With no signal

Every mark is saved on the phone first, with the time it happened, and sent when there is signal. A banner shows how many are waiting. Tap **Send now** to try straight away. Keep marking as normal. Nothing is lost.

## In an emergency

Press and hold **Hold for SOS** for two seconds. Your school is told straight away, with where the bus is. An SOS made with no signal is kept and sent as soon as the phone reconnects.

## Good to know

- Turn on **Share my location** so the school office can see where the bus is on its trip screen.
- If your school has transport alerts switched on, guardians get a message when their child boards, is dropped off, or does not board.
- Only students assigned to your route are on the list. If a child is missing, ask the office to check their route. See [Set up a bus route and its stops](/help/set-up-a-bus-route).

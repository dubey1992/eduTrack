This article explains how to take your class's daily register, on the web or in the School365ai staff app on an Android phone, and what happens when your phone has no signal.

## Who can do this

The class teacher of a section takes its register, once a day. A School Admin can also take or correct any register in the school. A teacher who is not the class teacher does not see that section.

## On the web

1. Open **Student Attendance** in the sidebar.
2. Pick the section in the **Class** list. The date shows today. You can change it to an earlier day if you need to.
3. Each student has a button for present, absent and leave. Choose **Mark All Present** to mark everyone present, then change the few who are absent or on leave.
4. Choose **Submit Attendance**.

## In the staff app

1. Open the app and go to **Home**. Your class has its own register card, showing Not taken until you take it.
2. Tap the card to open the register.
3. Tap **Present**, **Absent** or **Leave** for each student, or tap **All present** to fill in everyone not yet marked, then change the exceptions.
4. Tap **Submit**. The button stays greyed out until every student is marked. You are asked to check the counts, then the register is sent.

## With no signal

The app saves the register on your phone first, then sends it. If there is no signal, the screen says Waiting for signal to send and the card on **Home** shows Saved, not sent. The app keeps trying on its own while it is open, and tries again each time you come back to it, so the register is sent once your phone is back online. It stays saved on the phone even if you close the app.

To take a register offline, you must have opened that class at least once with signal, so the app has the class list on your phone.

If the school refuses a register, for example because the day is a holiday, the card shows Not sent. Tap it to see why.

## Correcting a register

On the web, open the same class and date, change the marks and choose **Update Attendance**. In the app, open the register and tap **Correct**, change the marks, then tap **Save correction**. Corrections need signal, because guardians may already have been told.

## Good to know

- A register can only be submitted once a day. After that you correct it rather than submit it again.
- Holidays and weekends are closed days, so there is no register to take.
- If your school sends absence alerts, guardians are told when the register is submitted. See [Send absence alerts to guardians](/help/send-absence-alerts-to-guardians).

This article explains how to mark your own attendance by checking in and out with the School365ai staff app when you arrive at school and when you leave.

## Who can do this

Any member of staff with an employment record at the school: teachers, HODs, office staff, accountants and transport managers. Your school must use staff attendance and have its location set on the map. Bus crew use the Driver app instead.

## Check in

1. When you reach school, open the app. The check-in card is at the top of **Home**.
2. Tap **Check in**.
3. The first time, your phone asks to let the app use your location. Allow it.
4. The app checks that you are within a set distance of the school, then records the time. The card shows when you checked in.

That marks you present for the day on the staff attendance register.

## Check out

1. When you leave, open the app and tap **Check out** on the same card.
2. Confirm with **Check out**.

If you check out again later, for example after staying late, the last check-out is the one that counts.

## If it does not work

- If you see **Turn on location**, your phone's location is switched off. Tap it, turn location on and try again.
- If you see **Open settings**, the app was refused location access. Tap it and allow location for the app.
- If you are told you are too far from school, move closer to the main building and try again. A weak location reading indoors can also be refused, so trying near a window or outside often helps.
- If there is no signal, nothing is recorded. Check in again once your phone is online.

## Good to know

- Your location is used once, to confirm you are at school. It is not stored.
- The distance is 100 metres from the school's pin unless your school has set a different one.
- If the office marked you absent before you arrived, checking in corrects it. If you are on approved leave that day, you cannot check in.
- Late is worked out from the time you check in and the school's first period. Read more about [staff attendance software](/staff-attendance-software).

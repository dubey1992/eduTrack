This article shows you how to have School365ai tell guardians automatically when their child is marked absent, and how to check that the messages went out.

## Who can do this

A School Admin sets this up on the web, from **Communication** in the sidebar. Messaging must also be available for your school. If you do not see **Communication**, ask School365ai to switch it on.

## How it works

When a class register is submitted, School365ai sends a message to the guardian of each student marked absent. It goes to the parent mobile number on the student's record.

A new school starts with absent-only alerts. Messages only reach phones once SMS delivery has been set up for your school, which School365ai arranges with you. Until then, the **Communication** screen tells you that messages are being recorded but not delivered.

## Steps

1. Open **Communication** and choose **Alert Settings**.
2. Make sure **Enable parent SMS** is on. Turning it off stops every text your school sends.
3. In **Send attendance alerts for**, choose **Absent only**. Choose **Present + Absent** if you also want guardians told when their child arrives, or **No attendance alerts** to stop them.
4. If your school uses them, you can also turn on **Enable WhatsApp** or **Enable email** to send a copy on those channels too.
5. Use **Send test SMS**, enter your own mobile number, and check the test message arrives.
6. Choose **Save Settings**.

## Change the wording

1. On **Communication**, choose **Message Templates**.
2. Find the message for a student marked absent and choose **Edit**.
3. Change the text. Under the message is a list of placeholders, such as the student's name. Tap one to add it to the end of the message, so each guardian sees their own child's details.
4. Choose **Save**. To go back to the original wording, choose **Use default**.

## Check what was sent

The **Communication** screen lists every message, with counts for today at the top. Use the **Status** and **Channel** filters to find a message, and **View** to see its details. If a message failed, choose **Send again**.

## Good to know

- A student with no parent mobile number on record gets no text. That message is listed as not sent, so you can spot missing numbers and add them in **Students**.
- Alerts go out when the register is submitted, so a register submitted early in the morning means guardians hear early too. See [Take the class register](/help/take-the-class-register).
- If you switch attendance alerts off, those messages are not listed at all, because nothing was meant to be sent.

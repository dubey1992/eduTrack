This article shows you how to set up a bus route: the stops it serves, the bus and crew that run it, and the students who ride it.

## Who can do this

A Transport Manager or a School Admin, on the web, from the **Transport** group in the sidebar.

## Before you start

Add the bus under **Vehicles** with **Add Vehicle**, and its driver and attendant under **Crew** with **Add crew**. You can add a route without them and fill them in later.

## Add the stops

1. Open **Bus Stops** and choose **Add Stop**.
2. Enter the **Stop name** and, if it helps, a **Landmark**, such as what you would tell a parent on the phone.
3. Set where the stop is. Search for the place or move the pin on the map, or type its **Latitude** and **Longitude**. The driver app uses this to guide the driver to the stop.
4. Choose **Save**, and repeat for each stop. A stop can be shared by more than one route.

## Add the route

1. Open **Routes** and choose **Add Route**.
2. Enter the **Route name**, for example Green Park.
3. Pick the **Vehicle**, the **Driver** and the **Bus attendant**. Each can be left empty for now.
4. Choose **Save**.

## Put the stops in order

1. On the route, choose **Stops**, then **Add Stop**.
2. Pick the **Bus stop**, set its **Order on route**, and add a pickup time and a drop time if you have them.
3. Choose **Save**, and repeat for each stop on the route. Choose **Done** when the list is in order.

## Assign students

1. Open **Students** and open the student's record to edit it.
2. In **Transport**, pick the route. Then pick the student's **Stop**.
3. Save the student.

Back on **Routes**, choose **Students** on a route to see everyone riding it, in stop order.

## Good to know

- Only students assigned to a route appear on the crew's trip list, so assign every rider before the first trip.
- If your school uses fee management and charges a bus fee for the route, a student is charged when they are assigned to it.
- Once the route is ready, the crew run it from their phone. See [Run a trip with the School365ai Driver app](/help/run-a-trip-with-the-driver-app).

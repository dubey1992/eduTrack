This article shows you how to record a fee payment a family has made at the school office, and how to print or email the receipt that goes with it.

## Who can do this

A School Admin or an Accountant, on the web, if your school uses fee management. School365ai records payments the office has already received, by cash, bank transfer, UPI, cheque, card or online transfer. Families do not pay through School365ai.

## Steps

1. Open **Student Fees** in the sidebar.
2. Find the student. Search by name or admission number, or narrow the list by **Class**. Turn on **Owing only** to see just the students who still owe.
3. Select the student to open their statement. It shows what they have been charged, what they have paid and their balance.
4. Choose **Record Payment**.
5. Enter the **Amount received**, or choose **Pay everything owed** to fill in the full balance.
6. Check the **Date received** and choose how the family paid in **Paid by**.
7. By default the payment goes to what is owed, oldest first. To pay particular installments instead, turn on **Choose the installments it pays** and enter an amount against each one.
8. Add a **Note (optional)** if it helps, such as a cheque number.
9. Turn on **Email the receipt to the guardian** if you want them to get a copy by email.
10. Choose **Record payment**.

The receipt opens straight away with its own receipt number. Choose **Download PDF** to print it, or **Email** to send it. Choose **Done** to go back to the statement.

## Find or reprint a receipt

Open **Receipts** in the sidebar. It lists every fee payment recorded, with its receipt. Use the filters to find one by how it was paid, or to show only valid or cancelled receipts. Select the student's name on a receipt to open their statement.

## If a payment was wrong

Receipts are never deleted. If a payment was recorded by mistake, or a cheque bounced, cancel the receipt from **Receipts** and give a reason. The amount goes back onto what the student owes, and the cancelled receipt stays on record with the reason.

## Good to know

- Amounts are always in your school's own currency, and each receipt keeps the currency it was recorded in.
- If a family pays more than they owe, the extra is kept as credit on the student's statement.
- The student's whole statement can be downloaded with **Statement PDF**.
- To set up what students are charged, see [Set up fees for a class](/help/set-up-fees-for-a-class). Read more about [school fee management software](/school-fee-management-software).

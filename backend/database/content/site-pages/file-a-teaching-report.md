This article shows you how to file a teaching report for a period you taught, on the web or in the School365ai staff app, and how your head of department reviews it.

## Who can do this

Teachers and HODs file reports for the periods they teach on the timetable. An HOD reviews the reports of teachers in their department. A School Admin can see every report in the school.

## In the staff app

1. Open the app and go to **Home**. Your periods for today are listed in order.
2. A period that still needs a report shows Report owed - tap to file. Tap it.
3. In **What was taught**, write the topic you covered.
4. If the lesson covered a topic on the syllabus, pick it in **Syllabus topic (optional)**. That topic is then marked as covered for the class.
5. Add **Homework (optional)** and **Remarks (optional)** if you have any.
6. Tap **File report**.

You can see the reports you have filed on the **Teaching** tab, under **Reports**.

## On the web

1. Open **Teaching Reports** in the sidebar. You see your scheduled periods and which ones have a report.
2. On a period without one, choose **Submit Report**.
3. Fill in **Topic taught**, and the syllabus topic, homework and remarks if you have them.
4. Choose **Submit Report**.

## If your report is sent back

Your HOD may send a report back with a note saying what to change. In the app, the period shows Sent back - tap to change. Open it, read the note, choose **Change and send again**, make your changes and tap **Send again**.

## For HODs: review a report

On the web, open **Teaching Reports** and choose **Mark Reviewed** on a report. In the staff app, open the **Department** tab, open a report, and tap **Mark reviewed**, or tap **Send back with a note** and say what should change. The teacher is told either way.

## Good to know

- You can file one report per period per day, and not for a period that has not happened yet.
- Your school may set a limit on how many days after the period you can still file. If the window has passed, the app tells you.
- No report is owed on holidays or on a day you are on approved leave.

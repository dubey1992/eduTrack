This article shows you how to reset your password if you have forgotten it, on the web or in the School365ai staff app.

## Who can do this

Anyone who signs in with an email address and password. Bus crew sign in with a mobile number and passcode instead, so see the last section.

## Steps

1. On the sign-in screen, choose **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter the **Email** address you sign in with.
3. Choose **Send Reset Link** on the web, or **Send reset link** in the app.
4. Open the email from School365ai and follow the link. It opens the **Reset Password** screen.
5. Type your new password in **New password** and again in **Confirm password**.
6. Choose **Reset Password**, then sign in with your new password.

## Choosing a password

Your new password needs at least 8 characters, with at least one letter and one number. Very common passwords are refused, even if they meet those rules.

## If the email does not arrive

- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Make sure you typed the email address your school has on record for you. For your privacy, the screen does not say whether an address has an account.
- The link works once and for one hour. If it has expired, start again from **Forgot password?** to get a fresh one.
- If it still does not arrive, ask your school office to check your email address.

## If your account is locked

After 10 wrong passwords in a row, your account is locked for 15 minutes. You can wait, or reset your password as above, which also unlocks it. A School Admin can unlock it for you too.

## Good to know

- Resetting your password signs you out on every other phone and computer, so anyone who knew your old password loses access.
- Nobody at your school can see your password, not even an administrator.
- Bus crew who forget their passcode should ask the school office for a new setup code. See [Run a trip with the School365ai Driver app](/help/run-a-trip-with-the-driver-app).

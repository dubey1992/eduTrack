This article shows you how to set a class test, enter the marks, and publish the result, on the web or in the School365ai staff app.

## Who can do this

Teachers set tests and enter marks for the classes and subjects they teach. HODs and School Admins can do the same. Only a School Admin or the subject's HOD can reopen a result once it is published.

## Before you start

Your school needs its terms set up for the academic year, and, if you want grades, a grade scale under **Grade Scales**. See [Set up your school](/help/set-up-your-school).

## On the web

1. Open **Class Tests** in the sidebar and choose **New Test**.
2. Pick the **Class**, **Subject** and **Term**, and the **Kind of test**, such as a unit test or a quiz.
3. Enter a **Title**, the marks it is **Out of**, and the **Date**. A **Pass mark (optional)** and a **Grade scale (optional)** can be added too.
4. Save the test. It appears on the list as a draft.
5. Choose **Marks** on the test. Enter each student's marks, or choose **Absent** for a student who missed it.
6. Choose **Save Marks**. You can come back and change them while the test is still a draft.
7. When every student has a mark or is marked absent, choose **Publish** and confirm.

If you already keep marks in a spreadsheet, use **Download sheet** to get the class list, fill it in, and bring it back with **Upload sheet**.

## In the staff app

1. Go to the **Teaching** tab and open **Marks**.
2. Tap **New test**, fill in the class and subject, title, marks and date, and tap **Set test**.
3. Enter each student's marks. Tap **Save marks** as you go.
4. When the class is complete, tap **Publish**, then **Publish** again to confirm.

## What publishing does

Publishing works out each student's grade from the grade scale and fixes it. The marks sheet closes, so nobody can change it by accident. If your school has result alerts switched on, guardians are sent their child's marks. Publishing cannot call back a message that has gone, so check the marks before you publish.

## Good to know

- Publish is only offered once everybody in the class has a mark or is marked absent.
- A test can be deleted while it is still a draft.
- Published results feed into each student's progress report for the term. Read more about [student progress reports](/student-progress-reports).
- If you spot a mistake after publishing, ask your HOD or School Admin to reopen the result.
